Bring your own papers

How it works

  1. PDFs are chunked with page + offset provenance (500 chars, 100 overlap)
  2. Hybrid retrieval: dense (MiniLM + FAISS) and BM25, fused with RRF
  3. Cross-encoder reranks the fused top-20 by joint relevance
  4. Answers are quoted sentences; below threshold → “insufficient evidence”
